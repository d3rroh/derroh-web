INC-2025-003 · CRYPTOMINER · RESOLVED

CentOS Cryptominer Eradication & Root Compromise Cleanup

CentOS cron.d CSPUpdate Bash Forensics rkhunter

A CentOS server was flagged after sustained CPU usage sat at 99%. Investigation traced the load to cryptominer binaries running under the name CSPUpdate, maintained through a malicious cron.d entry and hidden binaries staged in /tmp.

99%CPU Usage
4Malicious Procs
0%Post-Clean CPU

Eradication

The compromise had root-level access, so I traced the full scope of what the attacker had touched rather than just killing the visible processes. All four malicious processes and their persistence mechanisms were removed, and I ran rkhunter alongside manual bash forensics to confirm no additional footholds remained.

Hardening

After cleanup, CPU usage returned to 0% baseline. I hardened the server against reinfection by locking down cron access and auditing for the initial entry point that allowed root compromise in the first place.
