INC-2025-004 · INTRUSION · RESOLVED

Active SSH Compromise & Backdoor Removal

SSH Forensics iptables fail2ban

Routine log review surfaced unauthorized SSH keys added to a server's authorized_keys, alongside a backdoored /tmp/.bashrc planted by an attacker identifying themselves as "Frada/Bang Hengker" — a common signature seen in opportunistic SSH brute-force campaigns.

Investigation

I audited every active and historical SSH session on the box to establish the attacker's timeline and scope of access, checking for lateral movement to other services and confirming no persistence had been planted outside the identified backdoor.

Eviction & Hardening

The unauthorized keys were removed, the malicious .bashrc was cleaned, and all existing SSH keys were rotated. I then hardened the SSH configuration and layered iptables and fail2ban rules to shut down the brute-force pattern that led to the initial compromise.
